01 01 // Bloom Season

Test, explore & plant seeds

You’re ready for something new. Preferably without spending three months building an offer people politely “love” but never buy.

Bloom is where you test ideas, watch the response and find the offer, message or opportunity worth taking further.

Small tests. Useful feedback. Give the idea some evidence before giving it your entire quarter.

02 02 // Integration Season

Stabilise, systemise & rest

Sales are happening. Things are moving. But apparently, so are you. Constantly.

Integration is where you strengthen what’s working, document the repeatable tasks and build the systems that make your business easier to run.

Make room to rest, reduce how much depends on you and prepare for growth without carrying every task into it.

03 03 // Sprint Season

Lock in, execute & maximise

The offer has sold. The message is landing. You have something worth backing.

Sprint is where you focus your time and effort on making the most of it.

Increase visibility. Make clear offers. Follow up. Review the numbers and adjust as you go.

The random project can wait. Give what’s working your full attention before getting distracted by your next brilliant idea.
